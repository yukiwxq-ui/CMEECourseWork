- Dataset: Data from the Autumn Bird Migration Survey in Mount Jiulong, China.


- Introduction: Mount Jiulong is a mountain in eastern China and is an important migration belt for many raptor species, including the Grey-Faced Buzzard. To better investigate the patterns of raptor migration in eastern China, we began conducting migration surveys in autumn 2023, and we are now progressing into the third year. Our migration survey is mainly conducted from September to November, lasting 2 months annually. In the past 2 years, we have observed spectacular records of raptors. The total number of raptors recorded at a single location last year (2024) exceeded 16,800, with the count of Grey-Faced Buzzards exceeding 13,300. However, due to climate change, the number of Grey-Faced Buzzards passing by Jiulong is verbrating each year with shifts of migration peak time. In autumn 2025, the temperature is abnormally higher than usual and we have seen the migration peak time postponed by around a week.
In addition to raptors, we also focus on recording the migration of swifts and have made some interesting findings. Through the survey, we also discovered some rare bird records, such as the Wood Warbler (Phylloscopus sibilatrix), which has almost no records in East Asia, and the Black Wood Pigeon (Columba janthina), which normally inhabits islands far from the mainland and rarely visits the mainland. These recordings again, strikes the importance of this survey. 
This CSV file contains all the raptor and non-raptor bird recordings from September 2023 to October 2025 by the survey and includes information on date, count, etc.


- Variables

Date: Date of the recording.

Time: Time of the recording. Each observation usually sums the count of each species over an hour's duration.

Count: Count of individuals.

Loc: Observation Location. We have conducted the survey at 3 locations: DGS, CS, and DCS. CS and DCS are temporary survey points in 2023 and 2025 and serve as supplements to the main recordings at DGS. DGS is the main survey point.

Category: Raptor / Swift / Others

Scientific: Scientific names of the birds.

CommonName: Common names of the birds.


- Data from: Hangzhou Bird & Ecology Research Institute
